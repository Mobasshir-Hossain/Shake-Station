How to Play

Pip lives in a Mars outpost inside a snow globe. The Sun pays you Sun Coins every day. Spend them on Oxygen, Greenhouse, Heat and Shield to keep Pip alive for 10 days.

There are never enough coins for everything. Unspent coins are saved in the battery (up to 12), so save before a storm. Watch the forecast!

When Pip fails, the Black Box Replay shows the chain of causes.

About the science: events are inspired by real Mars dust storms and solar flares (NASA tracks flares in its DONKI database). Numbers here are simplified for gameplay and are not real measurements.
